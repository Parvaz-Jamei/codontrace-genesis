## Framing (from Phase 1)

**Question:** In worlds that differ in appearance and history but share a causal structure on the contact/ATP ledger, can a population retain **short, composable, falsifiable rules** that work in unseen worlds — and does survival pressure alone sustain that abstraction, or are memory and social teaching required?

Stronger than “a symbolic regressor recovers an equation offline” and stronger than “behaviour transfers within one world.” It asks for **evolutionary selection and inter-generation transfer of transferable symbolic causal laws**, plus an explicit boundary where an apparently true law is only a **simulator shortcut** (private features that do not travel with the shared causal structure), measured at run level with generation-boundary series.

**Phase-2 job:** map published precedent, mark deferred adjacent (offline equation discovery; wake–sleep program synthesis; geometry proving), fix the exact design gap, and propose falsifiable interdisciplinary combos — without authorising runners or claiming uniqueness.

## 2) Exact gap for Phase-2 design

**What the literature already does well**

- Offline symbolic regression recovers equations from fixed datasets (Schmidt & Lipson 2009; Brunton et al. 2016; Rudy et al. 2017; Udrescu & Tegmark 2020).
- Compositional program synthesis builds transferable symbolic libraries across tasks (DreamCoder: arXiv `2006.08381`; journal `10.1098/rsta.2022.0050`).
- Multitask / multi-environment **GP** transfers instances, features, or shared trees across related regression tasks (Chen–Xue–Zhang line; Liang et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2024).
- Social learning can evolve under survival pressure with imitation error as a key parameter (Gonzalez et al. 2017).
- Neuro-symbolic geometry proving is a strong adjacent methods benchmark (Trinh et al. 2024) — deferred here.

**What Phase 2 still needs and was not found as a packaged published design**

| Phase-2 requirement | Status in published record |
|---|---|
| Evolutionary selection of **short composable falsifiable causal symbolic laws** (not offline fit) | Offline SR / GP multitask exist; **not** embedded as survival-selected law candidates on a ledger with generation-boundary retention. |
| Transfer scored on **held-out worlds** sharing causal skeleton, different appearance | GP cross-domain generalisation on datasets exists; **not** interventional accuracy on held-out worlds with declared shared skeleton. |
| **Survival-only** vs **memory / social teaching** arms | Gonzalez gives survival-selected social learning of behavioural variants; **not** law-memory / teaching of falsifiable symbolic rules with interventional assays. |
| Pre-registered **simulator-shortcut probe** (private features vs shared structure) | Shortcut / private-correlate language appears in causal ML preprints; **no** joint evolutionary multi-world protocol with teaching cut + shortcut probe. |
| Unit \(N\) = **runs**; generation-boundary series; domain-free ledger | CodonTrace-specific; not addressed. |
| No infection in `engine.py`; no Euler-in-loop; no Avida-into-engine; no mega-utils; no second pop engine | Standing locks; literature Avida / second engines are **not** imported. |

**Gap wording (phase-2; matches Phase-1 open boundary):**

We are not aware of a published precedent for a pre-registered multi-world protocol in which a population under survival pressure retains short composable causal rules that transfer to held-out worlds with shared structure, where transfer is scored by interventional accuracy (not train-world fit), where survival-only is contrasted with memory / social teaching, and where a simulator-shortcut probe shows when an apparently true rule depends on engine-private features rather than the shared causal structure.

Absence of a hit in the present search is **not** proof of uniqueness. The claim ceiling remains `phase2_design`.

## 3) Solved elsewhere? **NO**

**Answer: NO** — not as the Phase-1/2 assembly.

**Why (concise):**

- **Offline SR / PDE discovery** (Schmidt–Lipson; SINDy; PDE-FIND; Udrescu–Tegmark) solve equation recovery from fixed data. Phase-1 explicitly **rejects** equating that with evolutionary multi-world transfer of falsifiable causal laws.
- **DreamCoder** solves compositional symbolic library learning and task transfer via wake–sleep synthesis. It is not a survival-ecology contrast of teaching vs survival-only with a shortcut probe on shared causal structure.
- **Multitask GP transfer** (Chen–Xue–Zhang; Liang; Wang) solves cross-task / cross-domain generalisation of regression expressions. Offline evolutionary computation on benchmarks ≠ held-out world interventional transfer under survival / teaching arms.
- **Gonzalez et al. 2017** solves when social learning evolves under survival pressure. It does not transmit short falsifiable causal symbolic laws or test multi-world shortcut probes.
- **Trinh et al. 2024 (AlphaGeometry line)** is adjacent geometry proving — deferred; does not settle the CodonTrace question.

**Caveat:** Ingredients are published. Claiming uniqueness or “first” would be improper. The live gap is the **integrated contrast design**, not the existence of any one ingredient.

## 4) Interdisciplinary combos A–E (falsifiable; domain-free ledger)

Each combo maps onto Phase-1 H0–H3. All must express worlds, laws, memory, and teaching only as contact/ATP ledger variables, generation-boundary retention or teaching of short symbolic candidates, held-out world swaps, and shortcut probes — **no** infection physics in `engine.py`, **no** Euler-in-loop, **no** Avida into engine, **no** mega-utils, **no** second population engine.

### Combo A — Offline SR recovery × multi-world interventional transfer cut

- **Inspiration:** Schmidt & Lipson 2009; Brunton et al. 2016; Rudy et al. 2017; Udrescu & Tegmark 2020 — offline equation recovery.
- **Mapping:** Same symbolic candidate class as offline SR, but selection and scoring occur only inside the evolutionary multi-world protocol; train-world fit alone is **invalid**.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** If an arm clears train-world fit ≥ 0.80 but held-out interventional accuracy drops ≥ 0.20, favour **H1** (world-specific heuristics). H2 requires held-out interventional accuracy ≥ θ (= 0.80) **and** shortcut-probe pass. Pure offline SR without the multi-world protocol → design FAIL for this question.

### Combo B — DreamCoder compositional library × generation-boundary teaching

- **Inspiration:** DreamCoder (arXiv `2006.08381`; `10.1098/rsta.2022.0050`) — compositional symbolic abstractions that transfer across tasks.
- **Mapping:** Short composable law candidates inherit or are taught at generation boundaries (Phase-1 §6.2–6.3); complexity bound and composition algebra registered in the design digest before any run.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Teaching / memory arms that retain composable candidates clear transfer-success share ≥ 0.15 above best non-transfer control with shortcut-probe pass rate ≥ 0.80 among successful runs (**H2**). If composition without teaching matches teaching, H3 fails. If no arm clears held-out threshold → **H0**.

### Combo C — Gonzalez survival-selected social learning × survival-only vs teaching arms

- **Inspiration:** Gonzalez, Watson & Bullock 2017 (`10.1162/artl_a_00244`) — social learning under strong survival selection; imitation error > mutation.
- **Mapping:** Survival-only arm = selection on reproductive / energetic success, no explicit law memory, no teaching events. Memory arm = lifetime retention of law candidates. Social-teaching arm = transmission at generation boundaries (Phase-1 §6.3).
- **Falsifiable prediction:** **H3** — memory and/or teaching clear H2 margin; survival-only stays at H0/H1. If survival-only matches teaching on held-out transfer within 0.15, H3 fails (survival may suffice under that cell). Advantage only via infection physics or domain RNG hacks → design FAIL.

### Combo D — Multitask GP cross-domain transfer × held-out causal-skeleton worlds

- **Inspiration:** Chen, Xue & Zhang 2022 (`10.1109/tcyb.2020.2969689`); Liang et al. 2025 (`10.1109/iceaai64185.2025.10956846`) — cross-domain / shared-tree GP transfer.
- **Mapping:** World family shares declared causal skeleton on contact/ATP ledger; appearance axes and train/held-out split registered before any run (Phase-1 §6.1). Transfer success = interventional accuracy ≥ θ on held-out subset by horizon T, scored per **run**.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Cross-appearance transfer that fails when the shared skeleton is relocated (even if surface cues remain) fails the shortcut probe → **not H2**. Transfer that clears held-out accuracy **and** passes the probe supports H2. Train-world-only generalisation (classic GP test-set) never counts.

### Combo E — Simulator-shortcut probe × private features vs shared structure

- **Inspiration:** Phase-1 §6.4 mandatory probe; causal-ML “shortcut / private correlate” language (adjacent watch only).
- **Mapping:** Held-out variant preserves surface cues or engine-private correlates while breaking/relocating the shared causal skeleton (or reverse). Candidate law **fails** the probe if it succeeds only when private correlates are present and fails when only the shared skeleton remains (or vice versa, as registered).
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Held-out success with shortcut-probe **fail** → discard as simulator shortcut; not H2. H2 support requires transfer-success share ≥ 0.15 above best non-transfer control **and** shortcut-probe pass rate ≥ 0.80 among successful runs. Empty probe in the design digest → reopen freeze.
