# Scanlan et al. 2015 — mutator / abiotic-constraint comparator

Scanlan PD, Hall AR, Blackshields G, Friman V-P, Davis MR Jr, Goldberg JB,
Buckling A (2015). Coevolution with bacteriophages drives genome-wide host
evolution and constrains the acquisition of abiotic-beneficial mutations.
*Molecular Biology and Evolution* 32:1425–1435.
doi:[10.1093/molbev/msv032](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msv032)

**Digital use in this port:** Phase 23 Scanlan mutator dual-null campaign
crosses elevated vs baseline mutation with coevolution vs abiotic arms at the
SemanticGenome layer. The digital assay is designed so
`elevated_mutation_under_coevolution_always_improves_abiotic` can fail.
`gene_identity_proved` and CRISPR identity stay False. Comparison is not wet
mutator-gene identity, not CRISPR genetics, and not phage-therapy guidance.
