# Open problem — time-shift FRQ vs ERQ discrimination (2026-09-25)

## Chosen problem (one)

**Can a dynamic life-loop engine discriminate oscillatory Red Queen (FRQ /
contemporaneous match peak) from escalatory arms race (ERQ / future-advantage
mono-rise) using an in-loop time-shift assay with content / structure / dual
nulls — without claiming `red_queen_proved`?**

## Why still open

Wet and theoretical literature show that short time-shift windows can look
directional even when the generative process is fluctuating selection
(Buckingham & Ashby 2022). Decaestecker et al. (2007) recovered a
contemporaneous infectivity peak from sediment archives; Brockhurst et al.
(2014) place fluctuating vs escalatory Red Queen modes in one framework.
On digital engines the hard part is not drawing a peak on a handmade graph —
it is archiving cohorts **during** a dynamic loop and letting null arms kill
the signature. Prior innovation-meter toys (β₁ on frozen graphs, Fisher on
fabricated maps, static DiD) are explicitly not the template.

## Mechanism innovation (loop-changing)

`codontrace.life_loop.time_shift_assay`:

1. **CohortArchive** — records opaque feature bags every tick (during the loop).
2. **Cross-temporal match panel** — past / contemporary / future partner rates.
3. **Structural classifier** — `peak_contemp` | `mono_rise` | `mono_fall` | `flat` | `other`.
4. **Generative mechanisms** — `match_linked_cycle` (tracking → peak) and
   `trait_escalation` (coverage climb → mono-rise), plus content / structure /

archive). No infection physics in `engine.py`.
