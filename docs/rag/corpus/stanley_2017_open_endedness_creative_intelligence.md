## Digest

Stanley frames open-endedness as a necessary component of creative intelligence, not a metric checked once. The argument builds on Lehman and Stanley novelty search and the OEE workshop tradition: interesting novelty should keep being generated without a researcher-supplied final objective. A QD archive that fills bins, a MODES novelty number after a persistence window, or a Channon step that recorded activity are instruments. They are not creative intelligence. If a system has not got open-endedness as a *result*, it has not got that component of creative intelligence. CodonTrace Genesis has the instruments and an explicit refusal (open_ended_intelligence forbidden). Paraphrasing MODES novelty as creativity is a claim violation.
