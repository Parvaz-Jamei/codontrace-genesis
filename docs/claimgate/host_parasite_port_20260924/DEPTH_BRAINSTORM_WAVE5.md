## Round 1 — Shared diagnosis: what Phases 1–17 leave weak

**A:** Mode completeness and genome×continuum are done. Journal-facing gaps
are **temporal coevolution dynamics** (ARD→FSD as a *protocol*, not an enum)
and **multi-seed contingency** under parasitism (Zaman complexity is not a
law; S1 still soft). Freeze/replay proves schedule control; it does not prove
repeatable complexity across seeds.

**B:** Disagrees that dynamics labels alone are enough. Wet literature’s
distinctive claims are (1) **costs of generalism** driving ARD→FSD
(Koskella & Brockhurst 2014; Hall 2011), (2) **resource supply** modulating
FSD (Lopez Pascua 2014), (3) **genome-wide / mutator** consequences of
coevolution that constrain abiotic adaptation (Scanlan 2015). Without those
digital honesty maps, COU remains repertoire theater.

**C:** Approves A and B only if journal grade means **V&V packet quality**:
every new attach prereg-bound; public ladder never rises; `red_queen_proved`,
`oee_type1_proved`, `major_transition_proved`, phage therapy / clinical /
CRISPR identity / human virulence stay blocked. Adds: Phase 9 Cornish pack is
still a **two-arm observational vs interventional** sketch — not a sequential
multi-intervention falsification suite. Soft-complete **attach registry** for
reviewers is missing as a first-class artifact.

**Forced disagreement:** A wants dynamics transition first. B wants
resource×FSD and mutator before “more Zaman.” C wants sequential Cornish +
registry hygiene before any new biology-flavored diagnostic. Round-1 lock:
Wave 5 is **not** more modes; it is **dynamics honesty + resource factorial +
contingency + Cornish deepening**, with registry soft-complete as Phase 18
earn-in hygiene — not taxonomy theater.

**Weaknesses entering Wave 5 (consensus):**

1. ARD/FSD are labels (Phase 5), not transition-protocol digests.
2. No resource × coevolution-dynamics factorial (Lopez Pascua analogue).
3. S1 contingency/repeatability under parasitism lacks multi-seed ClaimGate attach.
4. Scanlan mutator / abiotic-constraint dual-null not shipped.
5. Cornish sequential multi-intervention deepening absent.
6. Soft-complete journal attach-registry packet absent.
7. MODES/Channon/Goldsby remain comparator-only (correctly).

## Round 4 — Falsification / dual-null / prereg; Expert C veto power

### Keeper 1 — ARD→FSD + cost-of-generalism

- **Prereg:** time-slice schedule, range metrics, dual-null arms declared.
- **Dual-null:** (i) shuffled interaction partners (structure-null),
  (ii) abiotic-only stress without parasites (content/partner-null).
- **Falsify:** “dynamics always ARD under parasitism”; attach allowed when
  slices pairwise-distinct **or** dual-null correctly fails the universal claim.
- **C veto:** `red_queen_proved` remains False forever; no wet infectivity
  identity; no phage-therapy language.

### Keeper 2 — Resource × ARD/FSD factorial

- **Prereg:** resource/ATP levels × parasite-present/absent × spatial optional.
- **Dual-null:** high-resource abiotic-only vs low-resource biotic.
- **Falsify:** “higher resources always increase FSD-like labels” (literature
  often finds the opposite — Lopez Pascua; digital assay must be able to match
  *or* fail without claiming wet replication).
- **C veto:** never claim wet resource–virulence proof; never clinical dosing.

### Keeper 3 — Multi-seed contingency (S1)

- **Prereg:** seed set, complexity/repertoire/genome observables, success
  criterion = digest distinctness / variance report — **not** “complexity rose.”
- **Dual-null:** parasite-absent multi-seed control.
- **Falsify:** “parasites always raise complexity across seeds.”
- **C veto:** `complexity_emergence_proved` stays False; no Avida-replacement.

### Keeper 4 — Sequential Cornish deepening

- **Prereg:** ordered intervention list; observational baseline arm required.
- **Refusal:** observational accuracy alone never sets `intervention_supported`.
- **Falsify:** twin that matches history but fails later intervention in schedule.
- **C veto:** no clinical decision-support claim; FDA/ASME language = COU labels only.

### Keeper 5 — Scanlan mutator dual-null

- **Prereg:** mutation-rate factor × coevolution/abiotic arms; genome digests.
- **Dual-null:** elevated mutation **without** parasites; parasites with baseline
  mutation; content-null genomes.
- **Falsify:** “elevated mutation under coevolution always improves abiotic
  adaptation” (Scanlan: coevolution can constrain abiotic-beneficial acquisition).
- **C veto:** no wet mutator-gene identity; no CRISPR; `gene_identity_proved`
  stays False.

**C global vetoes (Wave 5):** any phase that raises public ladder; any
`oee_*_proved` / `red_queen_proved` / `major_transition_proved`; Goldsby MLS
engine; DomainProfile split; `engine.py` infection; BAIC pin edits;
AI/agent meta in docs; invented DOIs.
