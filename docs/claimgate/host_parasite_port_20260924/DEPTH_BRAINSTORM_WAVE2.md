# Design workshop — wave 2 depth (after Phases 1–6)

Three reviewers (digital evolution; empirical phage–bacteria / symbiosis;
causal/V&V methodology) assessed what Phases 1–6 still leave unfinished relative
to the grounded REQUIREMENTS / CHALLENGES package. Citations stay within
CITATIONS.md and the evidence/ notes. Disagreement is recorded. Ends with
Phases 7–9.

## Shared diagnosis after Phases 1–6

Delivered and useful: fail-closed HOST_PARASITE profile; env outside engine;
dual-null; multi-seed digests; freeze/replay *arm*; spatial inject; real
vertical + mixed; ARD/FSD-like range labels; abiotic×biotic factorial;
adaptive-origin gate; network digests; mandatory prereg; DOI evidence stubs.

Still short of a journal-grade *campaign suite*:

1. **Zaman freeze vs replay vs reciprocal coevolution** is not yet three
   distinct arms with host-complexity analogues (Zaman et al. 2014
   doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.1002023). Current freeze_replay collapses freeze
   and replay into one arm.
2. **Parasitism–mutualism continuum** (Symbulation / Gupta–Vostinar 2021
   doi:10.3389/fevo.2021.739047; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.6380543) is named but
   not a first-class interaction-value axis with VT×spatial factorial.
3. **“Parasites always raise evolvability”** is not yet a dedicated
   falsification assay (Scanlan/Buckling MBE constraint; Challenge S5).
4. **Cornish-style intervention packs** remain single-shot score deltas, not a
   preregistered multi-arm observational-vs-interventional campaign that
   ClaimGate refuses to promote (Cornish et al. JMLR 27(152) 2026 /
   arXiv:2301.07210).
5. **External comparator matrix** (Avida parasites, Symbulation, CRISPR-phage
   models as literature-only comparators) is thin; needed so reviewers see
   what is *compared* vs what is *claimed identical*.
6. No **HARD_EXPERIMENT-style host–parasite campaign pack** with locked digests
   under docs/ (additive; BAIC pins untouched) that other researchers can
   replay.

Innovation target (locked): ClaimGate-auditable coevolution *protocols* —
freeze ≠ replay ≠ reciprocal; continuum honesty; evolvability falsification;
intervention refusal — not another Avida clone.

## Round 1 — What a reviewer still rejects

**Digital evolution:** One freeze_replay arm cannot speak to Zaman’s main
result (reciprocal coevolution > freeze or replay alone). Need three arms and
a host “repertoire richness” analogue with digests.

**Empirical / symbiosis:** Without an interaction-value continuum and a
vertical-transmission sweep under spatial structure, the port still looks like
obligate theft only, not the continuum Symbulation exists to study. Never brand
mutualism as “success.”

**Causal/V&V:** Observational matching must not unlock intervention_supported.
Need a multi-arm intervention campaign object whose ClaimGate attach refuses
ceilings when only observational arms exist (Cornish).

## Round 2 — Where novelty lives now

**Digital evolution:** Three-arm Zaman protocol + dual-null + digests as a
*library* surface.

**Empirical:** Continuum × VT × spatial factorial that can show mutualism is
optional, not assumed.

**Causal/V&V:** Evolvability-constraint falsification + Cornish pack wired to
the same prereg schema from Phase 6.

**Build order:** Phase 7 (Zaman three-arm) → Phase 8 (continuum + VT×spatial)
→ Phase 9 (evolvability falsification + Cornish pack + comparator matrix +
HE_HP campaign artifact). Genome richness still waits until audit protocols
exist.
