## Digest

Szathmáry and Maynard Smith (Nature 1995, doi:10.1038/374227a0) listed major evolutionary transitions: independent replicators becoming chromosomes, prokaryotes becoming eukaryotes, asexuals becoming sexuals, protists becoming multicellular organisms, solitary individuals becoming colonies, primate societies becoming human societies with language. Each transition creates a new level of individuality. Michod's export-of-fitness / evolutionary transitions in individuality (Darwinian Dynamics; PNAS 2007 104:8613-8618, doi:10.1073/pnas.0701482104) requires that fitness be exported to the higher-level unit and that lower-level conflict be suppressed (often via germline sequestration). A tagged deme whose mean fitness is recorded is not a new Darwinian individual. Rank-selecting demes is not conflict suppression. Round-robin soma/germline labels are not an evolved transition. Goldsby 2012's loss of lower-level autonomy under task-switching costs is the kind of *result* this literature demands. CodonTrace Genesis currently records group fitness and forbids collective_intelligence and major_transition_in_individuality.

## Key claims

- A major transition creates a new Darwinian individual, not a new data structure.
- Export-of-fitness plus conflict suppression (often germline) are mechanistic requirements.
- Group mean fitness without those mechanisms is group selection bookkeeping.
