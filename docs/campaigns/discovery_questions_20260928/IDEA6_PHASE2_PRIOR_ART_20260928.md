## Framing (from Phase 1)

**Question:** Can evidence-based epistemic restraint — the rule “I do not know; experiment or consult before acting” — invade and persist as a **heritable decision trait** when acting on weak hypotheses harms self and neighbours? When does correct restraint remain selectively stable, and when does it collapse under deception pressure that lacks structural support (consult / re-test / sanction channels)?

**Operational contrast (mandatory):**  
- **Restraint** = ledger-visible withhold-and-experiment/consult when evidence strength is below a locked threshold.  
- **Bold-action** = act on best current hypothesis below threshold, paying weak-hypothesis harm.  
- **Deception pressure** = misleading evidence/advice/signals inducing harmful action under low evidence.  
- **Structural support** = ledger channels that make restraint viable (verification, re-test, sanction, unforgeable shared evidence).  
- **ClaimGate ≠ trait.**

---

## Bottom line

1. **Published ingredients exist** for costly ignorance, reproductive value of information, social-learning transitions, veil-of-ignorance cooperation, learning costs under change, epistemic vigilance, tactical deception under conditional cooperation, and honest-signaling support via punishment — all Crossref-verified below.  
2. **The Phase-1 assembly was not found as a packaged design:** a pre-registered coevolution of **evidence-threshold restraint** with **deception/cooperation pressure**, scoring **lineage survival-share at run level**, with an **explicit collapse cell** when structural support is cut, on a contact/ATP ledger, **distinct from researcher-side claim auditing**.  
3. **Solved elsewhere?** **No** as that assembly. **Yes** as scattered near-misses (individual VOI/ignorance; social-learning invasion; ignorance-as-cooperation constraint; vigilance/deception arms races; sanction-supported honesty). Absence of a hit is **not** uniqueness.  
4. **Phase-2 work** is therefore **mechanism-competition / cell design** (Combos A–E), not rediscovery of VOI or social learning. Ceiling remains `phase2_design`.

## Cluster readings (what each cluster does / does not cover)

### C1 — Ignorance evolutionarily beneficial

Field & Bonsall show that once **collection cost** is charged against reproductive value, VOI can be **negative**, so remaining ignorant can maximise fitness. McNamara–Dall and Pike–McNamara–Houston establish the **cost-free** baseline (VOI ≥ 0; continuous-action expression). Donaldson-Matasci–Bergstrom–Lachmann link information value to fitness/bet-hedging.

**Near Idea 6:** justifies *when not to buy evidence*.  
**Miss:** individual optimality under abiotic/own-payoff structure; **not** a heritable “withhold → experiment/consult” trait under **neighbour harm** + **deception** + **support-cut collapse**.

### C2 — Value of information evolution

Same VOI core plus Dall et al. review of animal information use. Arehart & Adler quantify when learning (memory α, exploration ε) **underperforms** fixed strategies in changing environments.

**Near:** cost clocks for experiment/consult vs bold action.  
**Miss:** coevolving deceivers; lineage survival-share; structural-support factorial.

### C3 — Social learning transitions (Gonzalez `10.1162/artl_a_00244`)

Gonzalez–Watson–Bullock: social learning can invade from instinctive actuators without sophisticated decision biases when behavioural variant space is large, survival selection is strong, and imitation error > mutation; genotype–phenotype disengagement signals cultural takeoff. Kharratzadeh et al. / Smolla et al. address Rogers-type free-riding and competition between social and individual learning.

**Near:** consult/imitate as evolvable channel; non-genetic inheritance of decision phenotypes.  
**Miss:** evidence-threshold restraint under deception; support-cut collapse; neighbour-harm harm schedule as primary estimand.

### C4 — Epistemic humility / restraint / cooperation

Queller–Strassmann: ignorance of role/relatedness can **constrain selfishness** and favour cooperation. Faria–Gardner: formal caveat — ignorance raises average cooperation only under concave relatedness–cooperation maps. Marshall et al.: empirical veil/fairness in mammals. Sperber et al. / Heintz et al.: **epistemic / strategic vigilance** as cognitive protection against misinformation — related language, but primarily **receiver skepticism**, not “I don’t know → experiment/consult before harmful action” as a **lineage trait** with neighbour-harm externalities.

**Near:** ignorance/restraint language in cooperative evolution; vigilance against bad testimony.  
**Miss:** pre-registered evidence-strength threshold + experiment/consult arms + bold rival + deception×support cells on a run-level ledger. Vigilance ≠ ClaimGate; vigilance ≠ Phase-1 restraint definition.

### C5 — Deception exploitation of cautious / conditional agents

McNally–Jackson: conditional cooperation creates selection for **tactical deception**. Mokkonen–Lindstedt review evolutionary ecology of deception. Sarkadi et al.: deception dynamics in multi-agent settings. Clark–Kimbrough: **opportunistic punishment / social structure** can sustain honest signaling. Tump et al.: error-management thresholds under asymmetric group mistake costs (collective bias risk).

**Near:** deceivers exploit conditional/cautious strategies; sanctions/structure support honesty.  
**Miss:** explicit **collapse when deception pressure lacks structural support** as a named coevolution failure mode for an **evidence-based restraint** allele/rule scored at **run-level survival share**.

## Combos A–E (domain-free mechanism mash-ups)

All combos must stay on contact/ATP ledger actions, evidence/threshold state, resource/survival schedules, and `GenerationBoundaryObserver` timing. **Forbidden:** infection physics in `engine.py`; ClaimGate-as-trait; Euler “identity” proofs; importing Avida organisms into the engine.

### Combo A — Costly VOI × neighbour-harm externality × bold rival

**Inspiration:** Field–Bonsall costly ignorance; McNamara–Dall / Pike VOI; Phase-1 weak-hypothesis harm.  
**Mapping:** Restraint pays consult/experiment cost \(c\) when evidence \(u < u^*\); bold skips \(c\) but risks harm \(h_{\mathrm{self}}+h_{\mathrm{nbr}}\).  
**Falsifiable prediction:** With deception off and moderate \(h_{\mathrm{nbr}}\), restraint clears ≥ 0.15 survival-share vs bold over ≥ 6 runs when \(c\) is below a pre-registered fraction of expected harm; if \(c\) is high and \(h_{\mathrm{nbr}}=0\), bold wins (H0-like). If restraint wins only by silent ClaimGate logging, design FAIL.

### Combo B — Social-learning consult channel (Gonzalez) × evidence threshold

**Inspiration:** Gonzalez–Watson–Bullock social-learning invasion; Kharratzadeh hybrid learners; Phase-1 consult action.  
**Mapping:** Consult = imitate/query a ledger-visible neighbour’s recent verified outcome; genetic bold vs genetic restraint-with-consult compete.  
**Falsifiable prediction:** When behavioural variant space is large and survival selection strong (Gonzalez regime), restraint-with-consult invades vs instinctive bold; when imitation is of **unverified** advice under deception, consult without verification loses ≥ 0.15 relative to verified-consult (support-on). Pure imitation without evidence threshold ≠ Phase-1 restraint.

### Combo C — Veil / ignorance-as-cooperation × active evidence restraint (contrast)

**Inspiration:** Queller–Strassmann veil; Faria–Gardner concave/convex caveat.  
**Mapping:** Two ignorance modes: (i) **passive veil** (cannot know role/relatedness); (ii) **active restraint** (can know evidence strength; chooses to withhold and test).  
**Falsifiable prediction:** Passive veil can raise cooperation under concave maps (literature), but **fails** H2’s deception×support design: only active restraint + support channels show the support-cut collapse. If passive veil alone matches restraint’s survival-share under deception, Phase-1 trait definition is under-specified (reopen freeze).

### Combo D — Tactical deception vs epistemic vigilance vs restraint-with-sanction

**Inspiration:** McNally–Jackson tactical deception; Sperber epistemic vigilance; Heintz strategic vigilance; Clark–Kimbrough punishment → honesty.  
**Mapping:** Deceivers send misleading evidence; vigilance alone discounts advice; restraint-with-sanction withholds **and** pays to verify/sanction.  
**Falsifiable prediction (H2 core):** In deception + **support-on** (verify/sanction available), restraint clears ≥ 0.15 vs bold and vs vigilance-only. In **support-cut** (channels removed), restraint loses ≥ 0.15 relative to its support-on share (or falls below bold) — named collapse. If restraint collapses **with** support still on, do not claim H2 as stated (Phase-1 falsifier).

### Combo E — Error-management thresholds under asymmetric neighbour costs (Tump) × changing-env learning costs (Arehart–Adler)

**Inspiration:** Tump et al. collective error management; Arehart–Adler learning cost/benefit.  
**Mapping:** Threshold \(u^*\) evolves under asymmetric false-positive/false-negative costs that include neighbour ATP/survival; memory/exploration costs compete with threshold restraint.  
**Falsifiable prediction:** When neighbour false-action cost ≫ individual collect cost, evolved \(u^*\) rises (more restraint) and lineage share of restraint increases; when environment change rate is very high (Arehart–Adler “learning hurts” regime) **and** deception is fast, **no** arm clears 0.15 (H0/H1-adjacent speed limit) — distinct from support-cut collapse.
