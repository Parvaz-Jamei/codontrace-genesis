## Framing (from Phase 1)

**Question:** When lineages transmit **reasons**, **failed interventions**, and **validity bounds** (not only successful outcomes or raw observations), can a population discover a pre-registered causal law that no single lifetime has enough ledger data alone to establish — and under what transmission regimes does error propagation or imitation reverse that advantage?

Stronger than “more individuals help,” “more pooled data helps,” or “shared memory helps.” It asks for a **cumulative, critiqueable, re-testable causal-knowledge channel** under limited lifetimes, contrasted against size, pooling, and imitation controls, with run-level replication and generation-boundary series.

**Phase-2 job:** map published precedent, mark deferred adjacent (preprints / offline equation-discovery collectives), fix the exact design gap, and propose falsifiable interdisciplinary combos — without authorising runners or claiming uniqueness.

## 2) Exact gap for Phase-2 design

**What the literature already does well**

- Social learning can evolve under minimal cognitive assumptions, with **imitation error** as a key parameter (Gonzalez et al. 2017).
- Human cumulative culture can exceed individual invention and often proceeds **without** accurate causal models (Boyd et al. 2011; Derex et al. 2019; Saral et al. 2026).
- Transmitting causal/technical content or theories can help early, be dispensable later, or **harm** via canalization (Osiurak line; Derex et al. 2019/2025).
- Group size and network connectivity modulate accumulation (Derex 2013; Derex & Boyd 2016).
- Classical theory predicts when imitation fails to raise fitness and when critical evaluation restores it (Rogers; Enquist et al.).
- Offline collectives can discover equations from pooled data (deferred: MCI preprint; CoEvo).

**What Phase 2 still needs and was not found as a packaged published design**

| Phase-2 requirement | Status in published record |
|---|---|
| Transmit **reasons + failed interventions + validity bounds** as ledger-visible packages | Human “theory transmission” exists (Derex 2019/2025); **not** schema with failed interventional assays + explicit validity bounds under digital limited lifetimes. |
| Target **causal law unreachable** from any single lifetime sample of size available under \(L\) | Conceptual “beyond one lifetime” (Boyd niche); **no** pre-registered information/empirical unreachability criterion crossed with package vs pooling arms. |
| Controls: lifetime-alone vs size-matched **pooling** vs **success-imitation** vs full package | Pieces exist separately; **joint** pre-registered four-arm contrast **not found**. |
| **Reversal cell** where error/imitation makes full transmission **underperform** pooling/asocial by locked margin | Canalization / Rogers / refinement paradox are H3-adjacent in humans/theory; **not** pre-registered digital H3 cell on discovery-share. |
| Unit \(N\) = **runs**; generation-boundary series; domain-free ledger | CodonTrace-specific; not addressed. |
| No infection in `engine.py`; no second pop engine; no Avida-into-engine | Standing locks; literature Avida/RL systems are **not** imported into engine. |

**Gap wording (phase-2; matches Phase-1 open boundary):**

We are not aware of a published precedent for a pre-registered, run-level contrast in which transmission of **reasons + failed interventions + validity bounds** yields discovery of a causal law that no single lifetime can establish from its own ledger sample, while cutting that transmission (but retaining raw data pooling or imitation of successes) removes the advantage — including regimes where error transmission or imitation **reverses** the collective benefit.

Absence of a hit in the present search is **not** proof of uniqueness. Claim ceiling remains `phase2_design`.

## 3) Solved elsewhere? **NO**

**Answer: NO** — not as the Phase-1/2 assembly.

**Why (concise):**

- **Human cultural-evolution experiments** (Derex–Osiurak–Saral) solve causal-vs-transmission and theory-canalization questions in microsocieties. They do not implement lifetime-limited autonomous evolutionary ecosystems, ledger-visible failed-intervention packages, or run-level discovery of a pre-registered causal law with pooling/imitation cuts and a reversal cell.
- **ALife social-learning evolution** (Gonzalez et al.) solves when imitation evolves and when imitation error matters. It does not transmit critiqueable causal hypotheses or test lifetime-unreachability of a law.
- **Rogers / Enquist / refinement / canalization** solve when social learning fails or harms. They motivate H3 but do not supply the CodonTrace four-arm design.
- **Offline equation-discovery collectives** (MCI preprint; CoEvo) solve collective symbolic recovery from datasets. Phase-1 explicitly **defers** them: no survival, no limited lifetimes as cultural generations, no failed-intervention/bound packages as the cultural channel.
- **RL generational accumulation** (Cook et al. 2024) solves skill/policy accumulation across generations, not interventional causal-law discovery with H0–H3 controls.

**Caveat:** Ingredients are published. Claiming uniqueness or “first” would be improper. The live gap is the **integrated contrast design**, not the existence of any one ingredient.

## 4) Interdisciplinary combos A–E (falsifiable; domain-free ledger)

Each combo maps onto Phase-1 H0–H3. All must express transmission only as ledger-visible packages, contact/ATP structure, and generation-boundary inheritance — **no** infection physics in `engine.py`, **no** Euler-in-loop, **no** Avida into engine, **no** second population engine.

### Combo A — Gonzalez imitation-error × Boyd “beyond lifetime” unreachability

- **Inspiration:** Gonzalez et al. 2017 (imitation error > mutation enables social learning); Boyd et al. 2011 (culture exceeds individual lifetime invention).
- **Mapping:** Full package arm transmits reasons + failed interventions + bounds; imitation arm copies successes only; lifetime-alone uses horizon \(L\); target law pre-registered as unreachable under \(L\).
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Under moderate imitation error, full package discovery-share exceeds lifetime-alone **and** imitation by ≥ 0.15 over ≥ 6 runs (H2). Under very high imitation error (Gonzalez-style), full package falls within 0.15 of imitation or below (H3 cell). If pooling alone matches full package, H1.

### Combo B — Derex theory-canalization × failed-intervention cut test

- **Inspiration:** Derex et al. 2019/2025 — inaccurate or useless theories canalize exploration; stripping theory content while keeping configurations changes outcomes.
- **Mapping:** Cut test = remove failed-intervention and bound fields, keep raw pooling (Phase-1 mandatory cut).
- **Falsifiable prediction:** If H2 claimed, cutting failed-intervention + bound fields must remove the ≥ 0.15 margin while pooling remains (positive content control). If margin survives the cut, package content was not doing the work (H2 fails).

### Combo C — Saral/Osiurak causal-early / cultural-late dynamics × generation-boundary series

- **Inspiration:** Saral et al. 2026 (causal helps early, fades); Osiurak et al. 2022 (reasoning reconstructs under degraded info).
- **Mapping:** Score discovery-share by generation-boundary blocks via `GenerationBoundaryObserver`; compare early vs late windows under full package vs pooling.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Full package leads pooling by ≥ 0.15 in early boundaries; lead shrinks below 0.15 by late horizon **or** remains ≥ 0.15 only when validity bounds are transmitted (bounds prevent late overfit). If early lead never appears, H0/H1.

### Combo D — Rogers/Enquist critical filter × revision rule in package schema

- **Inspiration:** Rogers 1988 (imitation free-riders); Enquist et al. 2007 (critical social learning); Phase-1 revision rule (retain/edit/drop).
- **Mapping:** Package schema includes mandatory revision rule peers can apply without domain RNG; reversal cell strips revision (blind accept) or elevates transmission noise.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Blind-accept full packages underperform pooling by ≥ 0.15 in the reversal cell (H3). Enabling revision restores full package above pooling by ≥ 0.15 (critical filter necessary for H2).

### Combo E — Connectivity/size demography (Derex) × information-volume matching

- **Inspiration:** Derex 2013 group size; Derex & Boyd 2016 partial connectivity; Phase-1 reject equating more N with cumulative causal knowledge.
- **Mapping:** Size-matched and connectivity-matched pooling/imitation arms with information-volume bounds equalised to full package bit budget.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Increasing population size or changing connectivity under **pooling-only** cannot match full-package discovery-share within 0.15 if H2 is true. If size/connectivity alone closes the gap, reframe as demography (H1) — design FAIL for cumulative-causal claim.

## 5) Storm Round-1 questions / search terms

### Round-1 critique questions

1. Does any published digital-evolution or ALife study transmit **negative interventional assays** and **validity bounds** as first-class cultural objects (not just success phenotypes or raw observations)?
2. Is there a pre-registered contrast where a causal law is **information-theoretically or empirically unreachable** under lifetime \(L\), yet reachable under multi-lifetime package transmission?
3. Which hits jointly include **pooling** and **success-imitation** controls **and** a high-noise/high-imitation **reversal** cell on the same estimand?
4. Do human theory-canalization results (Derex 2019/2025) already settle H3 for CodonTrace, or do they only motivate the reversal cell without run-level ledger packages?
