## Digest

Conlin, Goldsby, Libby, Skocelas, Ratcliff, Ofria, and Kerr (bioRxiv 2023.03.15.532780) show that reproductive division of labor promotes the *entrenchment* of multicellularity in Avida. Simple multicellular groups evolve readily but revert; subsequent mutations that differentiate germ and soma make unicellular revertants unfit because unicells cannot circumvent a metabolic-productivity vs information-fidelity trade-off. When DoL is disincentivized, entrenchment does not increase. Related AvidaMT software implements tissue accretion vs propagule production as distinct instructions. - DoL can entrench multicellularity by making unicellular revertants unfit.
