## The only admissible novelty claim

A **causal mediation** claim: at fixed intervention time, function recovery differs between a
*named scaffold edge cut* and a *degree/ATP/edge-count-matched random cut*, and that difference
is attributable to **which organisms held the cut contact edges** (endpoint-specific realised
contact loss) rather than to a **global cost penalty** applied to every organism. The
distinguishing prediction is the refusal test: if both cuts have the same realised contacts and
the same population trajectory, or if the only difference is the penalty magnitude, the
mediation claim is dead. The D-2 pack measures that refusal before any window claim.
