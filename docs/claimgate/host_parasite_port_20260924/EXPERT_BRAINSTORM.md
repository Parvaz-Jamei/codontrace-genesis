## Round 1 — What should MVP *be*?

**Q1.** Is a ClaimGate DomainProfile (no infection physics) scientifically
useful, or theater?

**A:** Useful *if* it fails closed on therapy language and forces COU
statements before anyone wires Avida-like parasites. Zaman et al. (2014,
doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.1002023) and Fortuna et al. (2021,
doi:10.3389/fevo.2021.750772) already show that *parameters* (CPU theft,
task-overlap, one parasite per host) are load-bearing; labeling claims first
prevents silent overclaim when those parameters are later coded.

**B:** Skeptical. Empirically, phage–bacteria + CRISPR dynamics are where the
scientific action is (Chabas/Westra PLOS Biol 2023
doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3002122; RS Interface 2024
doi:10.1098/rsif.2024.0195). A profile that only blocks strings does not
reproduce punctuated immune dynamics. Still: blocking `phage_therapy_cleared`
and `vaccine_efficacy_proved` is non-negotiable hygiene.

**C:** Agree with A on gating, with B on humility. Cornish et al. (JMLR
27(152) 2026 / arXiv:2301.07210) show observational accuracy ≠ counterfactual
correctness. MVP should be: (1) profile + blocked claims, (2) a *declared*
intervention menu for later falsification, (3) no clinical certificates. FDA
2023 CM&S / ASME V&V 40-2018 risk commensurate evidence applies as **labels**,
same biomedical port stance — not clearance.

## Round 2 — Digital vs wet mapping

**Q2.** Which Avida/Symbulation mechanisms are mandatory analogies for Genesis?

**A:** Minimum viable *future* env (not this PR): obligate resource theft,
task-overlap infection eligibility, one infection seat per host, horizontal
injection (Fortuna et al. 2021 doi:10.3389/fevo.2021.750772). Complexity /
evolvability under coevolution (Zaman et al. 2014
doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.1002023) is a *target phenomenon*, not a guaranteed
outcome. Symbulation’s vertical transmission → mutualism tendency (Gupta,
Vostinar et al. 2021 doi:10.3389/fevo.2021.739047; Zenodo
10.5281/zenodo.6380543) belongs in v2 knobs, not MVP physics.

**B:** Task-overlap is a weak map to receptor/CRISPR specificity. CRISPR type
structure and eco-evo feedbacks (doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010329;
doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3002122) will not fall out of CPU theft. Do **not**
claim Genesis MVP “models CRISPR.” Use empirical papers as external
comparators and as sources of *falsifying* signatures (e.g., punctuated
spacer dynamics, doi:10.1098/rsif.2024.0195).

**C:** Mapping discipline: every digital mechanism gets a COU sentence and a
negative control. HE02 content-null / capsules_shuffled logic (Floreano 2007
doi:10.1016/j.cub.2007.01.058; Knoester 2008 doi:10.1145/1389095.1389130) is
the right pattern: destroy payload content while preserving interaction
slots. Okasha & Otsuka (2020 doi:10.1098/rstb.2019.0365): Price-like
summaries ≠ causality — so “parasite presence correlates with complexity”
must not be sold as an intervention effect without an arm.

**A (rebuttal):** Content-null is fair, but Fortuna’s infection rule is
contentful (task overlap). A pure shuffle may be too strong a null for
infection eligibility. Prefer: (i) shuffle parasite *payload*, (ii) keep or
break overlap separately — two controls, not one.

**B (agree/disagree):** Agree with two controls. Also add an abiotic-only arm:
Scanlan/Buckling-line MBE 2015 (academic.oup.com/mbe/article/32/6/1425)
shows coevolution can constrain abiotic-beneficial mutations — a digital port
that only celebrates “parasites increase evolvability” would be one-sided
relative to that empirical constraint result.

## Round 3 — Virulence, resistance, multilevel

**Q3.** Should the port expose “virulence” metrics?

**B:** Only as *digital* labels with blocked human/clinical aliases. Gandon
(doi:10.1098/rspb.2000.1100) qualitative vs quantitative resistance and
Alizon et al. (doi:10.1098/rstb.2013.0365) social infections are theory
priors — not licenses for `virulence_optimized_for_humans`.

**A:** In Avida, “virulence” ≈ CPU stolen / host fecundity hit (Fortuna et al.
2021). Fine as an internal metric name in a future env; ClaimGate must still
block clinical re-labeling.

**C:** Multilevel caution: host–parasite coupling can look like a major
transition (Okasha Front EcoEvo 2022 doi:10.3389/fevo.2022.793824; Michod &
Nedelcu 2003 doi:10.1093/icb/43.1.64 fitness reorganization). Innovation:
require an explicit *fitness-reorganization worksheet* (who’s fitness? host,
parasite, collective?) before any “transition” claim — analogous to
biomedical PIRT/weakest-submodel discipline, but for multilevel units.

## Round 4 — Expert C innovations (not yet standard in digital HP)

**Q4.** What should CodonTrace add that Avida/Symbulation platforms rarely
institutionalize?

**C1. Intervention falsification suite for the digital twin**
Pre-register interventions (remove parasites; freeze host evolution; break
spatial structure; content-null payloads) and require the twin to fail or
pass under those interventions — not only match observational trajectories
(Cornish et al. JMLR 27(152) 2026 / arXiv:2301.07210).

**C2. Risk/COU-commensurate evidence ladder for coevolution claims**
Reuse FDA 2023 CM&S + ASME V&V 40-2018 *language* as declared model-influence
× decision-consequence bars (biomedical port pattern) so a blog-plot of
“richer parasites” cannot claim epidemic or therapy relevance.

**C3. Dual nulls: structure-null × content-null**
Combine HE02-style content shuffle (Floreano 2007; Knoester 2008) with a
structure-null (randomize interaction partners / break task-overlap), and
treat Price-style aggregates as non-causal summaries (Okasha & Otsuka 2020
doi:10.1098/rstb.2019.0365).

**A:** C1–C3 are welcome *protocols*; they must not delay the thin profile.
Symbulation already varies transmission and spatial structure (Zenodo
10.5281/zenodo.6380543); what is new is making falsification + dual nulls
**ClaimGate-auditable**.

**B:** C1 matters most for CRISPR-adjacent storytelling: without intervention
tests, a digital “immune” module will be curve-fit to
doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3002122 figures and over-trusted.

## CONSENSUS DESIGN — CodonTrace Genesis host–parasite port

### MVP (this PR / immediate)

1. Register `HOST_PARASITE` DomainProfile with fail-closed clinical/therapy/
   epidemic/BSL/intelligence blocks; export thin adapter
   `bundle_from_host_parasite_cou` + `assert_claim_allowed`.
2. Docs pack under `docs/claimgate/host_parasite_port_20260924/` with grounded
   citations; **no** changes to BAIC pins or HE01 numbers.
3. Engine stays domain-agnostic; **no** infection physics in `engine.py`.
4. Declare COU/risk labels only (`asme_vv40=complement_only`,
   `fda_2023=declared_labels_only`, `infection_physics=not_implemented`).
5. Unit tests: blocked claims raise `ConfigurationError`; domain label
   `host_parasite` independent of biomedical.

### v1 (next engineering slice)

1. Optional `HostParasiteEnv` **outside** engine core: task-overlap eligibility,
   single seat, horizontal inject, configurable steal fraction (Fortuna et al.
   2021 doi:10.3389/fevo.2021.750772).
2. Dual null campaign template: content-null payload + structure-null overlap
   break (HE02 / Floreano 2007 / Knoester 2008 + Expert C).
3. ClaimGate bundles from declared digital scores with replay digests; still
   no clinical claims.

### v2

1. Transmission-mode + spatial-structure knobs (Symbulation / Gupta–Vostinar
   2021 doi:10.3389/fevo.2021.739047; Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.6380543).
2. Intervention falsification protocol documented against Cornish et al.
   (arXiv:2301.07210 / JMLR 27(152) 2026).
3. External comparator notebooks vs CRISPR–phage signatures
   (doi:10.1371/journal.pbio.3002122; doi:10.1098/rsif.2024.0195) —
   comparison, not identity claims.
4. Multilevel fitness-reorganization worksheet before any “transition” claim
   (Michod & Nedelcu 2003 doi:10.1093/icb/43.1.64; Okasha 2022
   doi:10.3389/fevo.2022.793824).
5. Explicit abiotic×biotic constraint assays inspired by Scanlan/Buckling MBE
   2015 (academic.oup.com/mbe/article/32/6/1425).

### Explicit non-goals (all versions unless separately certified elsewhere)

- Vaccine / antiviral / phage-therapy / epidemic-forecast / BSL / human
  virulence-optimization claims.
- Baking medicine or CRISPR genetics into `engine.py`.
- Committing untracked `src/codontrace/genesis/population/` junk.
