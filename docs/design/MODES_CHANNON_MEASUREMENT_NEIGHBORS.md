# MODES and Channon 2024 as measurement neighbors

Coordinator literature search: 2026-09-12.

## MODES (Dolson et al. 2019)

MODES is a **measurement toolbox**: change, novelty, complexity, and
ecological potential after a persistence filter. CodonTrace Genesis
exposes MODES-style *measurement* hooks. Those numbers are not a claim
pass.

## Channon 2024 Tokyo Type 1 procedure

Tokyo Type 1 is a **measurement-step procedure** (activity, novelty,
shadow/normalization, multi-replicate decision vocabulary). Recording
the steps — including `tokyo_type1_measurement_only` — is not
`tokyo_type1_passed`.

## ClaimGate rule

ClaimGate must **not** auto-grant OEE or Tokyo Type 1 from metrics
alone. The standalone auditor warns
`modes_and_channon_tokyo_type1_are_measurement_neighbors_not_a_pass`
and keeps `tokyo_type1_passed` forbidden.

See [`CLAIMGATE_STANDALONE.md`](../CLAIMGATE_STANDALONE.md) and
[`../WHY_NOT_INTELLIGENCE_YET.md`](../WHY_NOT_INTELLIGENCE_YET.md).
