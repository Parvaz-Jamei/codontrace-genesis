# RQ-1 metric split

The archived time-shift matrices are the **class-balanced assay**. The
analysis builds the antagonist side from the keys of
`antagonist_class_frequencies` and does not use the values as weights.
Equal exposure of the classes that happen to be present is not the pressure
of the living composition.

| Name | What it is | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| `class_balanced_assay` | one weight per present parasite class | the population's abundance |
| `frequency_weighted_abundance_pressure` | each class weighted by its count | ATP a host was observed to pay |
| infinite reserve | a standardised assay outside the population | living-history ATP |
| frozen exactly zero | often the debit multiplied by zero | biological or causal validity of the metric |

Intended pressure, paid ATP, contact opportunities and lineage growth are
four fields. Lag, rarity advantage, reciprocal feedback and the path-cut
control are four checks. None of them sets `hypothesis_supported` or
`red_queen_proved`.

Confirmatory `n` is the number of seeds. Generations inside a seed are not
independent replicates. The archived matrices are not recomputed here.
