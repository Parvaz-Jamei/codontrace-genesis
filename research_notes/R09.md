## 1. Literature & Theoretical Foundation

### 1.1 Hazen et al. (2007) & Functional Information
- **Citation:** Hazen, R. M., Griffin, P. L., Carothers, J. M., & Szostak, J. W. (2007). *Functional information and the emergence of biocomplexity*. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS), 104(suppl 1), 8574–8581. DOI: [10.1073/pnas.0701744104](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0701744104).
- **Core Principle:** Functional information quantifies the fraction of sequence configurations that achieve a specified biological or computational function: \(I(E_x) = -\log_2(M(E_x)/N)\).
- **Application to Reference Models:** Challenge 5 of the frontier suite evaluates functional information and neutral percolation via Monte Carlo exploration over abstract Hamming-distance spaces. This is an analytical reference model, distinct from full codon execution.

### 1.2 Full Engine vs Mathematical Reference Model Distinction
In evolutionary computation and ALife (e.g., Avida, Tierra, CodonTrace Genesis):
1. **Full GenesisEngine (`codontrace.genesis.engine.GenesisEngine`):**
   - Implements full biological assays, virtual-CPU instruction set / codon decoding, host-parasite contact ledgers, ATP transfer dynamics, and pedigree tracking.
   - High computational cost per generation; strict state determinism and replay verification.
2. **Frontier Reference Models (`scripts/grand_frontier_*.py`):**
   - Independent mathematical exploration benchmarks (OEE Novelty, MLS Price Equation, Mutualism Transition Barrier, Historical Contingency Replay, Hazen Functional Information, Eigen Quasispecies, Bedau Activity Shadow, Fisher Geometric Model).
   - Execute directly on dedicated CPU cores with PRNG-based state transitions to explore multi-day macro-evolutionary dynamics.
   - Must not be conflated with the GenesisEngine VM substrate.
